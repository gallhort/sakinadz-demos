A trading house built on direct lines.

Tamarisk Trade is a small Algiers team of buyers, logisticians and document specialists. We stay deliberately compact: the person who quotes your order is the person who follows it to delivery.

The company

Who we are.

We connect foreign suppliers with Algerian buyers, and Algerian producers with buyers abroad. Our job is to remove uncertainty: who the supplier is, what is really in the container, which document is missing, when the goods will be on site.

The name comes from the tamarisk, a tree that grows from the Mediterranean coast to the Saharan oases and holds firm where little else does. It suits a company that works across the whole country and well beyond it.

How we work

Four rules, no exceptions.

  1. Everything in writing

    Prices, Incoterms, lead times and responsibilities are confirmed by e-mail before any payment.

  2. One contact per file

    You deal with one named person from the first quote to the signed delivery note.

  3. Bad news travels first

    A delay or a quality issue is reported the day we learn about it, with the options available.

  4. No promise we cannot check

    We do not announce transit times, certifications or capacities that we cannot document.

Markets

Where our goods come and go.

Algiers sits a short crossing from southern Europe and at the start of the routes to West Africa. These are the regions we work with; specific countries are discussed case by case.

  • Region 01

    Southern & Western Europe

    France, Spain, Italy and the North Sea ports: equipment, spare parts and packaging in, agri-food out.

  • Region 02

    Türkiye & Eastern Mediterranean

    Building materials, textiles and light machinery.

  • Region 03

    Gulf & Middle East

    Re-export hubs and regional distributors.

  • Region 04

    Asia

    Industrial supplies and equipment, with inspection before loading.

  • Region 05

    Americas

  • Region 06
